A little preparation helps

What should I have ready when I call the vet?

A few details close at hand make any conversation with a vet quicker and clearer. Here is a simple checklist to use before you phone or email RM Solutions.

  1. Your animal's details

    Their name, species, breed if you know it, and roughly how old they are.

  2. What you've noticed

    Any changes in eating, drinking, energy, breathing or toileting, and when you first noticed them.

  3. Current medicines

    The names of any medicines, supplements or special food they're on, and the doses.

  4. Earlier records

    If you have notes from a previous vet, keep them nearby so you can answer questions about their history.
